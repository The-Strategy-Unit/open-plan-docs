Assuring the quality of the OpenPlan Hospital Capacity Model involves assessing whether
we've built the right model (:lucide-ticket-check: 'validation'), and whether we've built the model right 
(:lucide-shield-check: 'verification').

The former involves interrogating real user problems, and forming solutions that
can deliver value from day 1.
We do this with real users so we avoid guesswork, bias and the need for rework.

Verification includes iterative in-depth assessment of what we build, ensuring that
it is correct, complete, functional and maintainable.

The key elements of quality assurance can be thought of as a combination of
- asking the right people
- at the right times
- the right questions

At the Strategy Unit we are uniquely capable of performing quality assurance 
thanks to the diversity of skills and experience held 'in house' by our multi-
disciplinary team.  

We ensure that we leverage the strengths of expert analysts, statisticians,
data engineers, user experts, strategists and more from the very beginning of 
a project and throughout.

Using our extensive networks across the NHS and beyond, we can call upon a 
diverse range of external authorities to embed their insights into our processes, 
which also helps us avoid 'marking our own homework'; real world experts validate
what we do.

All this is planned, detailed and documented openly on our GitHub organisation, 
so stakeholders are empowered to understand more deeply the integrity of our 
products; they don't need to 'take our word for it' that the product is Quality 
Assured. 

## OpenPlan Hospital Capacity Model Quality Assurance Elements

:lucide-ticket-check: Method devised by lead analysts with iterative input from capacity 
conversion experts and hospital planners

:lucide-shield-check: Code interpretation of method verified by lead analyst

:lucide-shield-check: Model outputs verified internally 

:lucide-landmark: Governance structure set up for ownership of key assumptions

:lucide-ticket-check: Assumptions validated by a panel of external experts

:lucide-shield-check: Model outputs verified by external experts

:lucide-shield-check: Release checklist to safeguard functionality and stability








- internal verification of model results
- release checklists that cover - 
- internal verification of documentation as accurate description of method/code
- external validation/verification of all assumptions, outputs - HCP, clinical, analytical 
- user interactions to verify value, accessibility, usability.
