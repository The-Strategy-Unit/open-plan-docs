# Explain my results

Your results are provided as an Excel workbook. 
The cover sheet explains what you can find in each sheet.

The results are contained in the `estimated_capacity_needs` sheet of the results 
workbook. Each row represents a different type of resource.

## Understanding the estimates

The OpenPlan [demand model](https://connect.strategyunitwm.nhs.uk/nhp/project_information/) 
is _probabilistic_, which means it produces a range of 
possible activity estimates rather than a single value. 
We convert each of these into an estimate of the capacity required.

We summarise these capacity estimates using three values:

**Mean** - the average capacity requirement across all the estimates generated by the model.
**P10** - 10% of the modelled capacity estimates are below this value.
**P90** - 90% of the modelled capacity estimates are below this value.

Together, P10 and P90 describe the middle 80% of the capacity estimates generated 
by the model.

For more information on uncertainty, see [the explanation here](../user-guide/methodology.md).

## Example 

Suppose your results for adult elective surgical beds are:

|                                 | p10  | mean | p90 |
|---------------------------------|------|------|-----|
| Adult elective surgical beds	  | 28   | 30   | 32  |



You could interpret this as
> The model estimates an average need of 30 adult elective surgical beds. 
Across all the estimates generated by the model, 80% fall between 28 and 32 beds.

The range reflects uncertainty in the modelled demand and shows how this translates into variation in the resulting capacity estimates.
